FEATURED INDEPENDENT PROJECT · APPLIED AI

Meet Priority Assist.

An NLP-assisted Jira ticket prioritization concept, built as a Jira Forge plugin during my Engineering Management graduate studies. It explores how teams can turn issue descriptions into more structured prioritization decisions.

Try an illustrative workflow ↓Discuss the project ↗
AIPROJECT
LAB
THE PROBLEM

Too many tickets. Too little context.

Backlogs contain mixed signals: urgency, business impact, blockers and vague descriptions. Prioritization can become inconsistent when these signals are scattered across issues.

THE APPROACH

NLP + human judgment

Explore text-based signals in issue descriptions and use them to support prioritization recommendations, while keeping ownership and final decisions with the delivery team.

THE PLATFORM

Jira Forge

Designed around the Jira workflow, with an emphasis on usable recommendations rather than replacing Scrum teams, product owners or established governance.
